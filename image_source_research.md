# Image source research log

## Licensing policy for this update

- Google Image results are treated as discovery only, never as evidence of reuse rights.
- Preferred assets: public-domain or CC0 images. Where a high-quality relevant public-domain image is unavailable, use a Wikimedia Commons image with a clearly stated Creative Commons licence and provide full creator, title, source, and licence attribution on the website.
- Exclude images labelled non-commercial (NC), no-derivatives (ND), unknown, or unclear.
- Do not imply that an illustrative image depicts Hesham Elshamy’s confidential project; pair the asset with an explicit illustrative-image note where used.

## Visual suitability review

The Köln-Nord image is a clean, high-angle cloverleaf interchange photograph with roads, ramps, vegetation and the urban edge clearly distinguishable; it is suitable as a general illustrative visual for Verkehrsanlagen, coordination and network-level road planning. The Lehrter See image clearly shows a row of HGV parking spaces at a German motorway service area and is a direct, non-confidential visual counterpart to the case study about truck-parking capacity and Kolonnenparken. Neither asset is described on the site as an image of the portfolio project itself.
